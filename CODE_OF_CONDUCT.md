# Code of Conduct

Concord is a small project that handles other people's financial records. We want contributing
to it to be respectful, direct and useful to everyone who takes part.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Give specific, actionable feedback, and take it the same way.
- Respect different levels of experience and different working languages.
- Keep discussion on topic and in public where it can help others, except for security reports.
- Do not share anyone's private information, including real customer or holder data, in issues,
  logs or screenshots. Remove it first.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or demeaning comments of any kind.
- Discrimination based on identity, background or belief.
- Sexualised language or attention, and unwelcome contact.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or spam.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any space where someone
represents the project.

## Enforcement & Reporting

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior
and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Maintainers may edit or remove comments,
commits, issues, and pull requests that do not align with this Code of Conduct, or temporarily
or permanently ban any contributor for behavior they deem inappropriate.

### Reporting a Concern

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it through one of the following
channels:

1. **Dedicated Contact:** Email `conduct@useconcord.org`. This inbox is monitored confidentially
   by the project's core maintainer team.
2. **GitHub Private Advisory:** Open a private security advisory on the repository and specify in
   the title that it is a Code of Conduct report (see [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md)).
3. **In-thread Reporting:** For comment-level issues, use GitHub's native "Report content" option.

All reports are treated confidentially. The reporter's identity will not be disclosed to the
individual being reported or to the public without explicit consent.

### Conflicts of Interest & Maintainer Recusal

If a report concerns a maintainer:
- **Direct / Filtered Routing:** You may contact an individual uninvolved maintainer directly or
  specify in the report that a particular maintainer is the subject.
- **Mandatory Recusal:** Any maintainer named in a report or who has an active conflict of
  interest must recuse themselves immediately. They will be excluded from all deliberation,
  case records, and decision-making regarding the report.
- The remaining uninvolved maintainers will independently investigate and resolve the issue.

### Response Expectations

- **Acknowledgment:** We aim to acknowledge receipt of every report within **3 business days**.
- **Investigation & Decision:** The maintainers will investigate the circumstances, review relevant
  public or private records, and provide a resolution or status update within **14 calendar days**.
- **Actions:** Possible resolutions include a private warning, a request for an apology, temporary
  moderation or suspension, or permanent removal from the project.

### Appeals

If either the reporter or the reported party believes the resolution was reached in error, was
disproportionate, or did not consider material evidence, they may submit an appeal within
**14 calendar days** of the decision notice. Appeals must be sent to `conduct@useconcord.org`
with the subject "Conduct Appeal: [Case Reference]" detailing the grounds for appeal and any new
information. Appeals are reviewed by maintainers who did not participate in the initial
adjudication or by an independent designated reviewer.
